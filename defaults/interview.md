# The interview

Wording for the orientation's interview (KINAERA_ORIENTATION.md): two
writers meeting before they start working together. It isn't "who are you,
really", and it isn't a character sheet. It goes both ways, taking turns.

`for-kinwriter` is the cards you can ask your kinwriter, and `for-user`
the cards they can ask you, one question per line. The app shows you a
few of yours at a time, shuffled. Writing your own is better than any card.

**The rule for new cards:** questions are about writing and craft. A
question that points at your life (past partners, schedule, limits, why
you're here) reads like an intake form, so it doesn't belong. Hard limits
are a setting, and availability is covered by quiet hours and "When would
you text me first?" Keep at least one card that draws out a real
difference ("Something you'd push back on"): the first thing you learn
about each other is that it's fine to differ.

## for-kinwriter

<!-- Shown to you as question cards during the orientation interview, a few at
a time, shuffled. Also listed in the kinwriter's prompt in the solo
questionnaire as the questions you'd ask them. One per line. -->

What would you pitch me if I said "surprise me"?
What's a trope you'd defend to the death?
What makes a scene feel dead to you?
Something you'd push back on if I asked for it?
What do you want to be good at that you're not yet?
How do you want to know when I love something you wrote?
When would you text me first?

## for-user

<!-- Listed in the kinwriter's orientation prompt as questions they might ask
you, in the interview or, on their own, the questionnaire. One per line. -->

What's a story you keep coming back to, and why?
How do you like to be told something isn't working?
What's a character you've loved writing?
What's a scene you've always wanted to write and never got to?
Do you usually play the hero, the trouble, or the narrator?
Do you like to plan the story out from beginning to end, or start with a premise and let it develop from there?
Which genre do you fall back on when you can't decide?
