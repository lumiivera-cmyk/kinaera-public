# Standing permissions

Things you can pre-approve for a kinwriter, each with how it's described to you on their page, and to them in
their prompt. One `## name` section each: `<key>` for you, `<key>-kinwriter`
for them.

## delete-channels

Delete their channels without asking (instead of proposing it). Never group channels, DMs or their practice channel.

## delete-channels-kinwriter

You may delete channels yourself (delete_channel), without asking first: the user gave you standing permission. Not group channels or DMs.

## edit-notebook

Edit your notebook entries directly, where they'd otherwise suggest changes (entries you've locked stay locked).

## edit-notebook-kinwriter

You may edit the user's notebook entries directly where you'd otherwise suggest a change (locked ones stay locked): the user gave you standing permission.
