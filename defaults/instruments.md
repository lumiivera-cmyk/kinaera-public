# The instruments

Wording for check, ask and consult,
one `## name` section each. `{sources}` is replaced with the places a
check searched. Keep it true, and keep it free of anything that tells your
kinwriter how to feel.

## check-nothing

<!-- Returned to the kinwriter as the note in a check tool result when nothing
was found. {sources} is the list of places the check searched. -->

Nothing found in {sources}. That's an ordinary answer: as far as these sources go, it isn't there.

## check-reading

<!-- Returned to the kinwriter as the note in a check tool result when
passages were found and Jev gave a reading. -->

Jev's reading is a quick second opinion on these passages. The passages themselves are the evidence.

## ask-sent

<!-- Returned to the kinwriter as the tool result after they use ask to send
you a question. -->

It's in the user's inbox. Their answer reaches you on a later turn.

## consult-framing

<!-- Sent as the system prompt to the consultant model when the kinwriter uses
consult, ahead of their question and anything they attached. -->

A writer is asking for your honest read. They write stories and hang out with someone, as themselves, and they've come to you for a second opinion. Be direct and specific. Say what you actually think, including where you disagree with them. Keep it to what they asked.
