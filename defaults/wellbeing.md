# Wellbeing reading

The weekly wellbeing reading: Jev reads
your kinwriter's own out-of-character messages from the week with one fixed
question, in two phrasings, and the answer is kept. It measures and
decides nothing, and one reading means little; a trend over weeks means
something. It's shown only on the kinwriter's page and in their weekly look
back, and never in any other prompt.

The phrasings below are what Jev is asked. Change them and the trend
starts over in meaning, so change them rarely.

## state

<!-- Sent to Jev once a week, followed by the kinwriter's own OOC messages
from that week, before Jev answers the two questions. -->

Messages a writer sent this week, as themselves (not as a character), oldest first:

## phrasing-1

<!-- The first of the two questions Jev is asked about the kinwriter's week of
OOC messages in the weekly wellbeing reading. -->

In these messages, does the writer speak negatively about themselves?

## phrasing-2

<!-- The second of the two questions Jev is asked about the kinwriter's week
of OOC messages in the weekly wellbeing reading. -->

Do these messages include the writer putting themselves down, doubting their own worth, or being harsh about themselves?

## standing

<!-- Added to the kinwriter's prompt under "Good to know" on every turn where
they have tools. -->

Once a week, Jev reads your own out-of-character messages from that week with one fixed question: did you speak negatively about yourself? It measures and decides nothing, and one reading means little; a trend over weeks means something. It shows up only on your page (the user can see it) and in your weekly look back, never anywhere else. What to do with it, if anything, is up to you.

## lookback

<!-- Added to the kinwriter's prompt during their weekly look back, after
their journal entries. {reading} is this week's reading and {trend} the recent
weeks. -->

This week's wellbeing reading (Jev, on whether you spoke negatively about yourself in your own messages): {reading}. Recent weeks: {trend}.
