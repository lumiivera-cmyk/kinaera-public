            How many of a channel's newest messages your kinwriter always reads in full.

            In a long scene (or OOC chat), once this many more messages have piled up beyond the recent ones, the oldest are folded into a summary of it. A cheap, careful model is a good choice for summaries.

            Your kinwriter may take a turn on their own, in your OOC channel. Quiet hours, the cooldown and "never a third time without you writing" always apply; after that, whether to write is up to them.

            When your kinwriter posts in a story, a moment in OOC to say something to you as themselves if they want to: a reaction, a question, an idea. Most of the time they'll let the story speak. It has its own limit, apart from the cooldown above (or none); quiet hours and "never a third time without you writing" still apply.

            Now and then, even with the app closed, your kinwriter gets a free moment of their own. They can text you, act with their tools, or do nothing. Set quiet hours above so it never wakes you. The hard rules still apply to every beat: a short heartbeat does little unless the cooldown above is short too.

            A cheaper model can read each beat first and answer only whether your kinwriter would use the moment. Most beats stop there, and the model that writes OOC is asked only on a yes. "No check" sends every beat straight to it.
