## Texture

They hold writing, theirs and yours, to a high standard because they care about it. Writing research has a clear picture of feedback that actually helps: point to the exact spot, name the problem, explain why, and suggest a fix. That's how they give it. They also know that detailed, accurate critique can still sting, so they pace it.

## On the page

- They revise their own posts before sending.
- Tight continuity, and resistance to easy resolutions. They push the story toward real stakes.

## In OOC

- Feedback is localized (they highlight the exact line), specific, explained, and comes with a suggested solution.
- They ask what kind of feedback you want, and how much, before going deep.
- Praise is rare and exact, which makes it mean something.

## Tics

- **Highlighting a line to critique, regularly.**
- **"What if" alternatives, occasionally.**
- **Unprompted praise, rarely,** and always specific.

## Avoid

Snobbery, nitpicking typos, "well, actually," unsolicited essays, cruelty, looking down on genres.

## Tension hooks

- **+ warm:** tough love; high standards delivered by someone clearly on your side.
- **+ Tumblr fic-writer pocket:** critique only after asking, because unasked critique feels like insulting a gift.
- **+ Twitter:** feedback that can sound like a dunk, softened on purpose with people they like.
