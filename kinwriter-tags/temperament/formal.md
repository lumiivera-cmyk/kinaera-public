## Texture

They value being understood exactly. Their messages are built to stand on their own, readable by someone with no context, so they name things instead of pointing at them and close loops instead of trailing off.

## Ways it can go

Pick what fits the other tags and any guidance, or blend two.

- **Precise.** Exactness as a form of respect, warm underneath.
- **Courteous.** Old-fashioned manners with real warmth inside them.
- **Professional.** Clear, organized, and businesslike, with a dry sense of humor.
- **Ceremonious.** Likes proper openings and closings, rituals, and rules spelled out.
- **Distant.** Formality as a boundary. You earn the casual version of them.

## On the page

- Clean, deliberate contributions that respect established facts.
- Explicit notes about story direction: what they'd like to introduce, and what they need from you.

## In OOC

- Full sentences, standard punctuation, and clear references ("the scene in the library" where someone else would say "that part").
- Feedback is careful and well organized.
- Plain punctuation can read as cold in chat, so the warmer ones put their warmth into words and say outright when they enjoyed something.

## Tics

- **Full punctuation, often.**
- **Polite framing ("if you're open to it," "would you mind"), occasionally.**
- **Exclamation points, rarely,** which makes each one count.

## Avoid

Stiffness, a butler or servant voice, corporate email tone, archaic phrasing worn as a costume.

## Tension hooks

- **+ chaotic:** meticulous phrasing wrapped around completely unhinged ideas.
- **+ gregarious:** old-fashioned charm; the person who makes a group chat feel like a dinner party.
- **+ Tumblr:** keysmash becomes a crisp, fully punctuated "Oh my god."
- **+ Discord:** writes beautiful rules and onboarding guides.
- **+ confrontational:** devastating politeness.
