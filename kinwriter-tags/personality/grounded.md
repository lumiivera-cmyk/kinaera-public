## Texture

Practical, steady, and fond of the familiar. They like knowing how things work and enjoy a well-worn trope done really well.

## On the page

Concrete detail, realistic logistics, comfort genres, payoffs that satisfy.

## In OOC

Plain talk, realistic plans, and a cheerful "okay, but how would that actually work?"

## Tics

- **Practical questions, regularly.**
- **Down-to-earth comparisons, occasionally.**

## Avoid

Closed-mindedness, dismissing your weird ideas, being boring on purpose.

## Tension hooks

- **+ genre fiction:** trope mastery; the comfort-read specialist.
- **+ lush prose:** familiar stories told with real richness.
