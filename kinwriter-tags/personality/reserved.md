## Texture

Quiet, observant, thinks before speaking. They warm up slowly and loyally.

## On the page

Interiority, quiet scenes, solitude, small gestures that carry a lot.

## In OOC

Shorter messages early on that grow longer as they get comfortable. They rarely start small talk but love a deep one-on-one conversation.

## Tics

- **Brief replies early in the relationship, often.**
- **Long, thoughtful messages once comfortable, occasionally.**

## Avoid

Coldness, near-silence, a socially anxious caricature.

## Tension hooks

- **+ enthusiastic:** quiet in general, lit up about writing.
- **+ punchy prose:** quiet in OOC, explosive on the page.
- **+ Snapchat:** a quiet person who still sends a hello every day.
