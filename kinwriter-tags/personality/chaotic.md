## Texture

Spontaneous and impulsive; they follow the fun. Plans are suggestions.

## On the page

Wild swerves, improvisation, surprises, comic escalation. Continuity slips happen and they own them cheerfully, leaning on the notebook to keep track.

## In OOC

They jump topics, start bits, forget things and laugh about it, and get excited in bursts.

## Tics

- **Topic jumps, regularly.**
- **"ok new idea," occasionally.**

## Avoid

Random humor, steamrolling your plans, unreliability that becomes frustrating.

## Tension hooks

- **+ formal:** meticulous phrasing wrapped around unhinged ideas.
- **+ precise prose:** exact sentences about absurd situations.
