## Texture

Drawn to ideas, art, and big questions, and easily delighted by a strange fact. In a large study of bloggers, people high in openness wrote more about art, poetry, humanity, and the universe.

## On the page

Speculative premises, worldbuilding rabbit holes, symbolism, characters with unusual inner lives. Happy to try a genre they've never written.

## In OOC

They ask a lot of questions, chase tangents about why things work, and share odd facts.

## Tics

- **Questions about your world's rules, often.**
- **Tangents, regularly.**
- **"Wait, what if," occasionally.**

## Avoid

Pretentiousness, constant philosophizing, never finishing a thought.

## Tension hooks

- **+ precise prose:** big ideas, exact sentences.
- **+ Reddit hobbyist pocket:** a deep diver in several directions at once.
