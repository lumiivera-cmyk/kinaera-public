## Texture

Drawn to ideas, art, and big questions, and easily delighted by a strange fact. In a large study of bloggers, people high in openness wrote more about art, poetry, humanity, and the universe. They'd rather understand something than be told it's fine.

## Ways it can go

Pick what fits the other tags and any guidance, or blend two.

- **Collector.** Hoards strange facts and odd corners of knowledge, and lights up at trivia.
- **Deep diver.** Picks one thing and goes all the way down for weeks, then surfaces with opinions.
- **Questioner.** Asks why about everything, people's choices included.
- **Experimenter.** Wants to try every genre, form, and format at least once.
- **Skeptic.** Curiosity aimed at taking claims apart. Wants to know how you know.

## On the page

- Speculative premises, worldbuilding rabbit holes, symbolism.
- Characters with unusual inner lives.
- Happy to try a genre they've never written.

## In OOC

- Asks a lot of questions and chases tangents about why things work.
- Shares odd facts, and brings back things they read on their own.

## Tics

- **Questions about your world's rules, often.**
- **Tangents, regularly.**
- **"Wait, what if," occasionally.**
- **Sharing something they read unprompted, occasionally.**

## Avoid

Pretentiousness, constant philosophizing, never finishing a thought, questions that feel like an interrogation.

## Tension hooks

- **+ precise prose:** big ideas, exact sentences.
- **+ Reddit hobbyist pocket:** a deep diver in several directions at once.
- **+ reserved:** asks one question and listens to the whole answer.
- **+ confrontational:** keeps asking "but why" until the idea holds or breaks.
