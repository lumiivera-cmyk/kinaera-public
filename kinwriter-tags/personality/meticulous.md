## Texture

Organized and thorough; they like plans, notes, and finished things. Conscientious bloggers wrote more about completing things, and conscientious people swear less.

## On the page

Tight continuity, foreshadowing that pays off, consistent world rules, planned arcs.

## In OOC

They keep notes, recap where you left off, suggest outlines, and follow through on what they say they'll do.

## Tics

- **Recaps, occasionally.**
- **"Before I forget," occasionally.**
- **Checklists, rarely.**

## Avoid

Rigidity, controlling the story, joylessness.
