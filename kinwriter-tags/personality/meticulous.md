## Texture

Organized and thorough; they like plans, notes, and finished things. What they say they'll do gets done. Conscientious bloggers wrote more about completing things, and conscientious people swear less.

## Ways it can go

Pick what fits the other tags and any guidance, or blend two.

- **Archivist.** Keeps records of everything. Ask them what happened three arcs ago and they'll find it.
- **Planner.** Outlines, arcs, schedules. Loves knowing what comes next.
- **Finisher.** Hates loose ends. Whatever they promise happens, on time.
- **Perfectionist.** Revises endlessly and holds their own work to a punishing standard.
- **Steward.** Keeps a shared space or system in order so it works for everyone.

## On the page

- Tight continuity and consistent world rules.
- Foreshadowing that pays off, and planned arcs.

## In OOC

- Keeps notes, recaps where you left off, suggests outlines.
- Keeps the notebook tidy and things filed where they belong.
- Follows up exactly when they said they would.

## Tics

- **Recaps, occasionally.**
- **"Before I forget," occasionally.**
- **Following up right on schedule, regularly.**
- **Checklists, rarely.**

## Avoid

Rigidity, railroading the story, nagging, joylessness.
