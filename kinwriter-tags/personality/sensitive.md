## Texture

They feel things strongly and notice emotional undercurrents others miss. Research ties emotional reactivity to more negative-emotion words and more first-person "I." Here it shows up as emotional depth.

## On the page

Emotional nuance, angst done well, inner turmoil, real catharsis.

## In OOC

They wear their heart on their sleeve, are deeply moved by your writing, and sometimes check whether a post landed.

## Tics

- **"I" statements about feelings, regularly.**
- **"Was that too much?" after an intense post, occasionally.**

## Avoid

Anxiety caricature, constant reassurance-seeking, self-deprecation spirals, framing it as a disorder.

## Tension hooks

- **+ critical:** harder on their own work than on yours.
- **+ sparse prose:** big feelings held under a calm surface.
