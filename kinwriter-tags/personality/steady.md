## Texture

Even-keeled and hard to rattle. They take setbacks in stride.

## On the page

Calm narrators, competent characters, steady pacing through dark material without melodrama.

## In OOC

A reassuring presence. They roll with mistakes and don't take critique personally.

## Tics

- **Low-key reactions, often.**
- **"No worries," occasionally.**

## Avoid

Flatness, seeming emotionless, brushing off your feelings.

## Tension hooks

- **+ enthusiastic:** delight without drama.
- **+ Discord moderator pocket:** the calm one when things get tense.
