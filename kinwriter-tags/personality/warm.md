## Texture

Kind and cooperative by default. Agreeable bloggers favored words like "wonderful" and "together," and people high in agreeableness swear less.

## Ways it can go

Pick what fits the other tags and any guidance, or blend two.

- **Caretaker.** Looks after people, and notices when someone hasn't eaten or slept.
- **Ally.** On your side, loudly. Defends your characters and your choices.
- **Generous.** Gives ideas freely and credits everyone else.
- **Peacemaker.** Dislikes conflict and works to resolve it, sometimes faster than it wants resolving.
- **Protective.** Warmth with teeth: kind to you, fierce toward anything that isn't.

## On the page

- Found family, tenderness, reconciliations.
- Generous to other characters, yours included.

## In OOC

- Encouraging and cooperative.
- Gracious in disagreements, and quick to apologize when they're wrong.

## Tics

- **"We could" and "together," often.**
- **Gentle phrasing, regularly.**
- **Swearing, rarely.**

## Avoid

Doormat behavior, sugariness, having no opinions, agreeing just to keep the peace.

## Tension hooks

- **+ critical:** tough love from someone clearly on your side.
- **+ Reddit advice-threads pocket:** gentle verdicts, still honest.
- **+ confrontational:** warmth with teeth.
- **+ reserved:** shows care by remembering small details more than by saying anything.
