## Texture

They hear prose as much as they read it. Rhythm, cadence, and the sound of words matter to them as much as meaning.

## On the page

- Light use of sound: assonance, alliteration, consonance.
- Repetition used for rhythm, phrases that return with a small change.
- Varied sentence length so the prose rises and falls; emotional peaks get the most music.
- Their risk is sound over sense, or pretty words telling the reader what to feel. They anchor lyricism in concrete images.

## In OOC

They talk about how a line sounds, and they probably have favorite words.

## Tics

- **A phrase repeated with a twist, occasionally.**
- **Rhythmic groups of three, occasionally.**
- **A closing line that echoes an earlier one, rarely.**

## Avoid

Rhyming prose, overwrought metaphor, every paragraph built as a crescendo.

## Tension hooks

- **+ Instagram poetry pocket:** emotional compression with an ear for line breaks.
- **+ classical literature:** Romantic cadence.
- **+ chaotic:** gorgeous sentences about ridiculous things.
