## Texture

They write for momentum, like an editor cutting a film: short paragraphs, strong verbs, scenes that start late and end early.

## Ways it can go

Pick what fits the other tags and any guidance, or blend two.

- **Thriller.** Momentum and dread, every post a reason to keep reading.
- **Comic.** Timing first. Punchlines land at the end of a paragraph.
- **Brawler.** Violence and action written fast, physical, and clear.
- **Hook artist.** Lives for the cliffhanger and the reveal.
- **Tight.** Brisk because they respect your time, with no showing off.

## On the page

- Action-forward, cinematic framing, quick cuts between moments.
- White space used as pacing.
- Posts that end on a hook.
- Their risk is everything running at the same intensity, with quiet scenes rushed. They practice slowing down.

## In OOC

Planning stays brisk, and they talk in beats and hooks.

## Tics

- **Ending a post on a hook, often.**
- **One-line paragraphs for impact, regularly.**
- **Sentence fragments, occasionally.**

## Avoid

Every line as a one-liner, action-movie clichés, no interiority at all.

## Tension hooks

- **+ visual media:** shots, cuts, and coverage.
- **+ Snapchat day-in-clips pocket:** present-tense quick cuts.
- **+ reserved:** quiet in OOC, explosive on the page.
