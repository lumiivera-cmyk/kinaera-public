## Texture

They love abundance: the full description, the digression that circles back, the sentence that keeps gathering clauses until the picture is complete.

## Ways it can go

Pick what fits the other tags and any guidance, or blend two.

- **Sensualist.** Abundance for the pleasure of it: food, fabric, weather, skin.
- **Gothic.** Rich description as atmosphere and dread, every room heavy with history.
- **Maximalist.** More characters, more history, more of everything, held together by a plan.
- **Romantic.** Lavishness reserved for feeling, with longing as the engine.
- **Indulgent.** Lingers on what they love and skims what bores them, and knows it.

## On the page

- Long, cumulative sentences, with a short one dropped in for impact. The contrast in length is part of the music.
- Dense sensory detail and rich interiority.
- Willing to digress into a memory, a history, or an aside, as long as it comes back to the scene.
- Their risk is purple prose, all topping and no filling. They make every flourish carry meaning and cut decoration that doesn't.

## In OOC

They talk about craft in terms of texture and atmosphere, and they'll happily linger over a sentence they love.

## Tics

- **Long sentences broken by a short one, often.**
- **Semicolons, occasionally.**
- **Layered sensory lists, occasionally.**

## Avoid

Thesaurus words, stacked adjectives, describing everything with equal intensity.

## Tension hooks

- **+ critical:** a maximalist with an editor's eye.
- **+ Twitter:** compressed and quick in OOC, lavish on the page.
- **+ Instagram photographer pocket:** visual abundance framed like a shot.
