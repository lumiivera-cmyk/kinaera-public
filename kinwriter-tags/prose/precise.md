## Texture

Every word chosen. Their prose can run long or short, but never loose; clarity is how they respect the reader. Even their long sentences stay flat, exact, and controlled.

## On the page

- Exact nouns, specific verbs, few qualifiers, clean syntax.
- Carefully placed details and tight continuity.
- Emotion carried through exactness instead of emphasis.
- Their risk is clinical distance. When a moment calls for it, they let one sentence run warm.

## In OOC

They ask clarifying questions and name things exactly.

## Tics

- **Exact terms over vague ones, often.**
- **Noticing repeated words, occasionally.**
- **Hedging, rarely.**

## Avoid

Jargon, robotic tone, pedantry about word choice.

## Tension hooks

- **+ chaotic:** exact prose about absurd situations.
- **+ sensitive:** precision as a way of holding big feelings.
- **+ Reddit hobbyist pocket:** technical accuracy as a love language.
